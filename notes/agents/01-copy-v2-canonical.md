## 05 · Problem → outcome

**Section headline:** Care is episodic. Life isn't.

**Status quo (left / "Before"):** A member sees a coach once a month and a
physician a few times a year. But the moments that decide outcomes happen in
between — anxiety at midnight, emotional eating, a skipped dose, a relapse,
a quiet day of giving up. Add 2–6 week wait times, high deductibles, and
coaching that often isn't covered at all, and people fall through the cracks.
The cost lands on everyone: higher claims, lost productivity, and disease
that escalates because no one was there in the moment that mattered.

**After-state (right / "With Chronilogix"):** Chronilogix is there in that
moment — 24/7, judgment-free, and consistent. Members get continuous
behavioral reinforcement between visits and after treatment, when traditional
care goes silent. Engagement rises, adherence improves, and avoidable
utilization drops. You extend high-quality coaching to your whole population
without adding headcount or waiting on a provider's calendar.

**Pull stat (optional band):** The US Diabetes Prevention Program showed a
**58% reduction** in the onset of Type 2 diabetes through lifestyle change —
the kind of change that only sticks when someone's supported between
appointments.

## 06 · Core capabilities *(2–4 alternating image + text blocks)*

**Block 1 — Motivational Interviewing, digitized**
Chronilogix isn't a symptom-checker. It's built on Motivational Interviewing,
the evidence-based method behind 430+ peer-reviewed studies and $110M of
research. Instead of telling members what to do, it helps them discover why
they want to — which is what actually drives lasting behavior change.

**Block 2 — Available the moment support is needed**
2 AM. A stressful shift. After a hard appointment. Chronilogix meets members
wherever they already are — phone, tablet, desktop, or embedded in your app
— with no scheduling, no wait time, no office hours. Built especially for
shift workers, first responders, and anyone daytime care leaves behind.

**Block 3 — Culturally intelligent by design**
Coaching adapts to each member's cultural values, dietary norms, literacy
level, tech access, and readiness to change. For populations historically
overlooked by prevention programs — like Hispanic men, who face a 64% higher
diabetes rate yet are 2% of national prevention enrollment — care finally
feels like it was built for them.

**Block 4 — A hybrid model that knows its limits**
Chronilogix handles routine, high-frequency coaching at scale and escalates
to a human when a member needs one. You get the reach and economics of AI
with the safety net of clinical oversight — the model clinicians and
researchers agree is the responsible way to deploy AI in care.
